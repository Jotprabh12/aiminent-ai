# Chapter 6 --- Remaining Pages & Content Strategy

**Project:** Aiminent AI Website V1\
**Document Type:** Product Requirements Document (PRD)

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 1. Objective

While the homepage and solution pages are responsible for generating
leads, the remaining pages establish credibility, improve SEO, answer
objections, and prepare the website for future growth.

Every page should reinforce trust and guide visitors toward booking a
consultation.

# 3. About Page

## Goal

Convince visitors that Aiminent AI is a reliable long-term automation
partner.

### Hero

Headline:

> Building Practical AI Automation for Growing Businesses

Supporting copy:

We help businesses automate repetitive work, improve customer
experiences, and scale operations with AI-powered workflows and custom
software.

Primary CTA: Book Free Consultation

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Tell the story in three phases:

-   Why Aiminent AI was founded.
-   Why businesses struggle with manual processes.

Every page should include:

-   Unique title
-   Meta description
-   Canonical URL

-   Breadcrumbs

# 14. Conversion Strategy

Every page should end with:

-   One strong CTA
-   One trust statement
-   Link to consultation booking

Avoid dead ends.
