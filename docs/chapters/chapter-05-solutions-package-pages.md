# Chapter 5 --- Solutions & Package Pages

**Project:** Aiminent AI Website V1 **Document Type:** Product
Requirements Document (PRD)

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 1. Objective

The Solutions and Packages section is the primary sales engine of the
website.

Its goal is to transform curiosity into trust by explaining business
problems, demonstrating measurable outcomes, and encouraging visitors to
book a consultation.

The content should avoid technical jargon and instead communicate
business value.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. Information Architecture

    Solutions
    │
    ├── AI Lead Engine
    ├── AI Sales Assistant
    ├── AI Property Consultant
    ├── Customer Lifecycle Automation
    ├── Marketing Automation Suite
    └── Custom AI Solutions

    Packages
    │
    ├── Starter
    ├── Growth
    └── Enterprise

Each solution should have its own landing page.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 3. User Journey

Homepage ↓ Problem Recognition ↓ Solution Page ↓ Business Benefits ↓
Automation Examples ↓ FAQ ↓ Book Consultation

Every solution page should naturally guide visitors toward scheduling a
consultation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 4. Standard Solution Page Layout

Each solution page must follow the same structure:

1.  Hero
2.  Business Problems
3.  Solution Overview
4.  Key Features
5.  Automation Workflow
6.  Business Benefits
7.  Supported Integrations
8.  Frequently Asked Questions
9.  Final CTA

Consistency across all solution pages is essential.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 5. Hero Section

Large headline focused on outcomes.

Example:

"Never Lose Another Lead."

Supporting copy:

Capture, qualify, nurture, and manage every enquiry automatically using
AI-powered workflows.

Primary CTA:

Book Free Consultation

Secondary CTA:

Schedule Live Demo

Hero illustration should visualize the automation process.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 6. Business Problems Section

Every solution page should begin by identifying customer pain points.

Example:

AI Lead Engine

Problems:

-   Slow response time
-   Lost enquiries
-   Manual data entry
-   Missed follow-ups
-   Lead leakage
-   Duplicate records

Use icon cards with concise explanations.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 7. Solution Overview

Explain:

-   What the solution does
-   Who it is for
-   How it improves operations
-   Expected business outcomes

Avoid implementation details.

Focus on measurable business value.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 8. Key Features

Each feature should include:

Icon

Title

Short description

Business benefit

Possible examples:

-   AI Qualification
-   CRM Sync
-   WhatsApp Automation
-   Appointment Scheduling
-   Reporting Dashboard
-   Analytics

Display as responsive feature cards.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 9. Automation Workflow

Visualize every solution as a business process.

Example:

Lead ↓

AI Qualification ↓

CRM

↓

WhatsApp

↓

Sales Team

↓

Closed Deal

Animate each step sequentially.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 10. Business Benefits

Instead of features, emphasize outcomes.

Examples:

-   Faster response times
-   Improved customer experience
-   Reduced manual work
-   Better lead tracking
-   Increased sales efficiency
-   Higher conversion rates

Use statistics placeholders where future client data can be inserted.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 11. Supported Integrations

Display integration cards.

Potential integrations:

-   WhatsApp Business
-   HubSpot
-   Salesforce
-   Zoho CRM
-   Google Sheets
-   Google Calendar
-   Gmail
-   Outlook
-   Zapier
-   n8n
-   Custom APIs

Each card should include an icon and short description.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 12. FAQ

Every solution page should answer:

-   Can this integrate with my CRM?
-   How long does implementation take?
-   Can workflows be customized?
-   Is support included?
-   Can existing systems remain unchanged?
-   Is employee training provided?

Use animated accordions.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 13. Final CTA

Large banner.

Headline:

Let's Automate Your Business.

Supporting copy:

Book a free consultation and discover where automation can create the
biggest impact.

Buttons:

Book Free Consultation

Schedule Live Demo

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 14. Packages Page

The Packages page should compare service tiers.

Display three cards:

Starter

Growth

Enterprise

Do not show pricing.

Instead, list:

Ideal customer

Typical business size

Included services

Implementation scope

Support level

CTA

Book Consultation

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 15. Package Philosophy

Starter

For businesses beginning automation.

Growth

For businesses scaling operations.

Enterprise

Fully customized automation ecosystem.

Position the packages around business maturity rather than budget.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 16. Cross-Linking

Every package should link to:

-   Related solution pages
-   Contact page
-   Consultation booking
-   FAQ
-   Industry pages

Improve navigation and SEO.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 17. Motion Design

Recommended animations:

-   Card hover elevation
-   Workflow progression
-   Scroll reveals
-   Icon micro-interactions
-   CTA hover states
-   Accordion transitions

Animations should remain subtle and premium.
