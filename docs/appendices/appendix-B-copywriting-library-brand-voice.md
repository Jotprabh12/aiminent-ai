Aiminent AI should sound:

-   Professional
-   Confident
-   Clear
-   Business-focused
-   Helpful
-   Practical

Avoid hype, buzzwords, and unrealistic promises.

# 17. Brand Messaging Rules

Always emphasize:

-   Business outcomes
-   Time saved
-   Operational efficiency
-   Better customer experience
-   Growth through automation

Avoid:

-   Fear-based messaging
-   Overpromising
-   Technical jargon
-   AI hype

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 18. Acceptance Criteria

All website copy should be:

-   Clear
-   Consistent
-   Action-oriented
-   Trustworthy
-   Easy to understand
-   Focused on solving customer problems rather than showcasing
    technology.
