# 1. Vision

Aiminent AI is an AI automation agency focused on helping businesses
automate repetitive work, improve lead conversion, and increase
operational efficiency.

The initial market focus is **Real Estate Agencies in India**, while the
architecture must be designed to expand into Healthcare, Finance, Legal,
Education, Hospitality, Manufacturing, and other industries.

The website should prioritize **converting visitors into booked
consultations**.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. Brand Positioning

Primary headline:

> AI Automation Agency for Growing Businesses

Supporting copy:

> We build AI-powered workflows, CRM automations, WhatsApp systems, lead
> management, AI assistants, and custom business software that help
> businesses save time, close more customers, and grow faster.

Avoid hype such as "AI Operating System". Emphasize practical ROI.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 3. Business Goals

1.  Generate qualified leads.
2.  Encourage "Book Free Consultation".
3.  Position Aiminent AI as a premium, trustworthy automation partner.
4.  Showcase Real Estate solutions first.
5.  Leave clear room for future industries.
